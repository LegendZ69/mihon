## Policy and preservation

The user removed the screen password/fingerprint and authorized ordinary waking/unlocking as needed. No additional lock-security changes are required. The new application preference ignores sound effects while preserving dialogue, narration, names, meaningful signs and titles. Explicit SFX classifications determine exclusion; unknown types and dialogue containing sound words are not suppressed by text or geometry heuristics.

New translations and newly selected reviews use their snapshotted content policy. Old queued jobs and pending review checkpoints retain their previous policy; editable global/series preferences missing the new setting adopt Ignore SFX. Selected review of an old page uses the current series preference without replacing its saved provider configuration. Duplicate scheduling retains attempts and policy. The original-plus-preview input, one-pass/two-attempt limits, revision guards and Undo remain in effect.

The shared renderer hides existing cached SFX locally, including their masks, without rewriting saved text, raw OCR, manual edits, geometry or review revisions. Enqueueing a cached chapter after a content-policy change does not resubmit it. Ignored SFX region retry is rejected before changing the job or dispatching a request. Pure Paddle and ordinary allowed region retry remain text-only. The additive JSON policy fields require no SQL schema migration; the new actual SQLite persistence method passed on K90 and the actual 16384-byte emulator.
