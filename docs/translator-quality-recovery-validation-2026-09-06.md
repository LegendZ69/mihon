## Implemented contracts

New translation settings enable automatic quality review by default, with `FULL_PAGE_WHEN_AVAILABLE` coverage and a maximum of two transport attempts. The alternative coverage is `TEXT_ONLY`; the attempt setting accepts one or two. **One review pass is fixed application policy**, not an official provider default or a loop that repeats until the model approves its own output. The provider, model, generation settings and OCR policy remain explicit in the job/settings snapshots.

Each review persists its image identity, source revision, complete baseline result, settings, attempt reservations, findings, state and resulting repair revision. Completed translations remain readable while review is pending or fails. A transport attempt is reserved before dispatch; interruption leaves it counted. Transient/rate-limit errors may consume the remaining bounded attempt after backoff. Authentication/configuration errors pause review. Content, limits, unavailable originals and exhausted attempts produce explicit attention or incomplete states. The gateway performs each token-count or generation operation once per reserved attempt; it does not hide an additional generation retry inside that attempt.

Old queued job snapshots that lack a `qualityReview` member decode with review **disabled**. Existing cached pages are not automatically backfilled. The inspector's explicit **Review selected page** action schedules the selected saved page; review-only work does not retranslate the chapter. Paused selected checkpoints retain their already-used attempts. Unrelated page reviews are excluded from that explicit selection.

Result replacement uses an expected-revision comparison in the database transaction. If a manual edit, region retry or another review changes the page first, the stale proposal is superseded rather than overwriting that result. Undo is allowed only while the repair revision is still current; it restores the retained baseline as a new saved revision. The inspector also prevents a stale draft from being saved and exposes save/discard controls.

A passed review is an AI assessment. A repaired review means a proposed revision was applied; it may retain findings and does not mean human approval. An unchanged response with findings becomes `NEEDS_REVIEW`. The queue supports quality-state filtering and the inspector shows findings, AI confidence estimates, attempts, visual coverage and the explicit **human passage review pending** label.

## Source integrity, coverage and editing

A visual review uses one original image and its saved result. It bypasses image preparation, crops and tiles, validates the original hash and request limits, and refuses an EXIF-rotated/mirrored or unreadably oriented source before dispatch. This preserves the existing result with an incomplete outcome rather than presenting a transformed or partial source as a complete page. Full-page orientation normalization is not implemented by this change.

Pure PaddleOCR and explicit `TEXT_ONLY` review send text without reading or embedding the source file. Their region identities, raw OCR and geometry must remain unchanged. Such reviews can assess text and reading order but cannot recover visually missing passages or establish complete visual coverage. AI and hybrid visual reviews may propose geometry corrections or new regions; the validator retains stable existing IDs and immutable raw OCR/measured detector and recognizer confidence. Added visual regions cannot acquire fabricated measured OCR scores.

All accepted polygons are finite, convex, nonintersecting, within original-image bounds and contain 3–32 corners. Included regions require distinct nonnegative reading orders. Existing raw transcription remains distinct from corrected transcription and translation. Region-only retries retain user corrections and saved geometry/style through the revision guard.

The inspector has independent source, boxes, masks and translated-text switches, zoom/pan, draggable polygon corners, explicit coordinate fields, quarter-turn text rotation and reading-order controls. Split editing requires an explicit source assignment and translation for **each** child. It retains one excluded parent with the original raw OCR and measured scores; children receive separate correction text, unique IDs and unavailable confidence. It never automatically duplicates the parent's paragraph into both children. These edits remain a draft until **Save manual edits**. Reader renderers are unchanged by the inspector preview. Preview decoding is bounded and may report a platform-format limitation; universal image-format preview support is not established.
